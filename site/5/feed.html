]]><![CDATA[Your Grace is Enough, Lord!]]>Tue, 06 Jun 2023 17:01:28 GMThttps://whengodmadeyou.weebly.com/motherhoodparenting/june-07th-2023
“But he said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’ Therefore I will boast all the more gladly of my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest upon me.” 2 Corinthians 12:9 (ESV)

Being a mom is one wild rollercoaster ride, am I right? From morning till night.. From working, managing all the household stuff (kanina lang pagkadating ko, inupuan ko yung mga titiklupin ko na hindi pa rin natatapos...) to taking care of our kids, it can be overwhelming and leave you feeling wiped out. But God leads me to this verse from Corinthians 12:9.

So, Paul, in the midst of his own struggles, had this awesome chat with God. Nabanggit nya yung "thorn in my flesh". He was dealing with it.  We can relate din. Like our weaknesses, difficulties, hardships na nararansan as a Mom. Paul begged God to take it away.

You know what God told him? He said, "Hey Paul, my grace is all you need. My power works best in weak moments." Ang galing ano? 

" For when I am weak, then I am strong!" Nabanggit din ito ni Paul sa 2 Corinthians 12:10b. Thank you Lord for this reminder!

Dear mom like me.. His grace is more than sufficient to carry you through all those crazy moments. When you feel like you're about to lose your mind or like you can't handle it anymore, that's when God's power kicks in. He loves to show up in our weaknesses and prove just how strong He is. So, opportunity  ito ng Lord to demonstrate his POWER!, WOW!!

You see, being an amazing mom doesn't mean having it all figured out or pretending to be perfect. Nope! It's about realizing that you don't have to do it all on your own. It's about admitting your weaknesses and leaning on God's grace. When you do that, you'll find the rest you've been longing for. So, thank you, Lord!!

God's grace isn't about demanding perfection from us. It meets us right where we are, messy house and all. It's His grace na tutulong sa atin.  God's grace helps us tackle the ups and downs of motherhood. So, go ahead and let His grace surround you and me! Bringing calm to the chaos and refreshing our tired soul.

When you embrace your weaknesses, you create space for the power of Christ to work in and through you. Your vulnerability becomes a superhighway for His strength and grace to flow to your kiddos and everyone else around you. It's in your weakest moments that God's power shines the brightest. So, Lord, here's the spotlight and here's my heart! Biglang napakanta naman ako ng, "Shine bright like a diamond.." hehe.. 

So, sama-samang i-release -  all those burdens we're carrying! and let's surrender them to the Lord. You don't have to do it all alone. God is right there with you, ready to carry the load. Trust in His amazing grace and find the rest you need in His loving arms.

Let's pray together:
Hey God, it's me, Mom. I come to You today with all my weaknesses and struggles. Thank You for assuring me that Your grace is more than enough for me.  Fill me up with Your strength, so I can be the best mom I can be. Let Your power rest upon me, guiding and comforting me every step of the way. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Remember, you've got God's amazing grace on your side. Trust in His sufficiency and find the rest you need. You're doing an incredible job!​
