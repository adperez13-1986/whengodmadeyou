I am Grace, the older sister of Ken (my only sibling), the child of Tatay Rowel and Inay Susan.
Even when we were young, I witnessed the hard work of Inay and Tatay every day. Even during Mom's childbirth sa akin, we faced challenges. Every passing day until now, the Lord showed how good He is to our family.

I remember Tatay is always saying, "God we serve is alive."," Buhay ang Diyos na pinaglilingkuran natin!".  And yes! We carried that Word with us until now that I have my own little family. He never fails; the Lord never leave us! The power and faithfulness of the Lord never went out of scene in our lives as a family.

A flashback comes to my mind of us all sleeping together on a double-sized bed. The three of us would be side by side, and one at the foot of the bed (maalin sa aming dalawa ni Kenny).  Ang sarap alalahanin yung mga humble beginnings that teaches us to remain in HIM and always trust the process. 

We grew up with Tatay working abroad. The first country he went to was Korea. It was the first time we had nice toys, yung may natunog!!! Hahaha... Until, Tatay came back.  Inay and Tatay sold goods using Tatay's tricycle as a rolling store. Siya ata ang nagpasimula noon sa Batangas. hehe.  Aba!! I Thank you, Lord, we were able to finish our studies, Ken and through this! Grabe ang pagsasakripisyo ng mga Inay and Tatay!

Tatay continued to work abroad, including Brunei. Then in 2009, Inay went abroad to Italy. It wasn't easy, but I was amazed by Tatay and Inay! Then, kami lang tatlo sa Pinas ng mga Tatay ang naiwan. Si kenny at ako then ang Tatay. Itinuloy ng Tatay yung kanilang pangongontrata ng granite!

After a few more years, Tatay followed, and it was just Ken and me in the Philippines. We got to see more of the reality of life and learned how to be responsible even without Inay and Tatay. We carried with us all the things we learned from the way our parents raised us.

We learned various things like cooking, grocery shopping, and what we loved the most, budgeting! It was like we needed to decide, Ken and I, what we should buy. We'd prepare the calculator and compute grams to know where we could save money. "Let's get this," "This is cheap," "Look, we can buy more because it's affordable." We learned so much that we still carry with us to this day.

We never felt abandoned by Inay and Tatay.  Yes, sometimes we think, "What if we were all together?" But given the circumstances, we are grateful for the opportunity that they were there for each other in Italy. Maybe it was an advantage because they left when we were already capable and grown. We could say that we could handle it. Sometimes I think, if they left when we were still in high school or when we were little, it would have had a different impact on us.

The Lord is so good. I thank God for what we have. We are not a perfect family. There are fights, arguments (especially between Ken and me), shouting matches (oh, we both get so annoyed). Maybe Ken and I can't live together again, it would be chaos for sure. Haha. But we love each other. The support is always there even until now that we have own families.  And he loves my children so much. Haha... They are even more spoiled by him. Tsk!

Anyway, thank you, Lord, for the small family I come from. If it weren't for the trials we faced in the past, I probably wouldn't be as strong. So, thank you, Lord, for the foundation that Ken and I have. Thank you to Inay and Tatay who are always there for us.

I am looking forward to the time when we will be together. And I claim that it will happen with the help of the Lord.

Thank you, Lord, napakabuti Mo!
May 2018 Singapore

2019 - Mom of Two

Sabi nila, kapag first time Mom ka, FIRST time ang LAHAT mong ma-eexperience. And it is just same kapag naging MOM of TWO ka na. Lahat din bago sa'yo, maaaring hindi man sa lahat ng pagkakataon, but knowing that you have a toddler and a baby on the other side. Ibang-iba ang galawan ng dalawa na sila. 

But thank God, for the strength, really, THE JOY OF THE LORD IS MY STRENGTH!!!

Above all, with all the chaos and hirap ng pagiging Mommy (temporary lang naman). I pray na magamit kami ng Dy bilang mabuting magulang para sa kanila. 

God, use us and give us wisdom sa pagpapalaki sa kanila. 
Declaring that our children will be MIGHTY WARRIORS OF GOD! And I believe this with ALL of my heart. They will grow up to fight for His Kingdom!
They will BE WORLD CHANGERS.

My Journey as a Mom

 Motherhood, well it is really a ministry! It has refined me, shaped me, and transformed me like nothing else in my life ever has.
​
It brought me to the feet of Jesus lalo na sa mga time na hindi ko na alam ang gagawin ko.
It stretched my faith, knowing na si Lord lang talaga ang makakatulong. Yung mga impossible ay kaya Niyang gawing possible. 
It is a daily test of patience, lalo na ngayon na kaliliitan pa sila. Of course, pag-lumaki sila, ibang level of patience naman.
It exposed my weaknesses, na hindi ko maexplain, na marerealize mo na lang na mahina pala talaga ako.
But, it brought out my strengths, ung lakas na "kaya ko pala", na "Yes, mahina ako," pero andiyan ang Lord to support sa ministry na ibinigay Niya.  And thankful for giving me a partner in life kaya gumagaan ang journey kong ito. 

Being First time Mom, since 2016,  nangangapa pa sa simula. Araw araw new learnings talaga, actually hanggang ngayon. 

With our motherhood surrendered to God, knowing that GOD's help is just there. We will see that it’s not our clean homes, prepared activities for our kids and other more that make us good mothers. But it’s the GRACE that God extends to us every day.  

Kung wala yung GRACE ng Lord, ang hirap! 

I praise GOD for HIS presence sa buhay namin, wisdom and guidance Niya araw-araw. 

My desire is for Him to be glorified in my new adventure as a MOM. 

The ministry of motherhood is the most important ministry ngayon sa buhay ko. Empower me, Lord. May I go after it with all of my heart. I pray to have JOY as I embrace it! All its ups and downs. All its ins and outs, because it is, in fact, the most important work I'll ever do.

“Children are not a distraction from more important work. They are the most important work.” C.S. Lewis

It is a ministry I must embrace, prioritize, and commit to because my kids are worth it and taking time to raise them well is worth any effort it may take.

It is not easy. We won’t always do it perfectly. I am not a perfect MOM. My prayer is that may I ALWAYS REMEMBER to lean into our Father’s voice to walk this out according to His will.

When GOD Made me as a Mother to my children, I know that it is by God’s GRACE. Thank you, LORD!
