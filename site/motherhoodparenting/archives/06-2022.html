As for me and my House, we will serve the Lord

8/6/2022

0 Comments

 
Ang sarap balikan yung nakaraan na mga bata pa kami. Tanda ko dati, kapag araw ng linggo, kailangan ay maaga kaming gumising para makapaghanda para sa pagpunta sa church. Tanda ko din, maaga pa lang, ang pang-gising sa amin ay malakas na tugtog na mga Christian songs.

Nakakatuwa, tanda ko, hindi pedeng absent kami. Sama-sama kaming magchuchurch, suot ang pinakamagandang damit pang-simba. At bago umalis ng bahay ay sama-sama kaming mag-prepray. Then, excited na sumakay sa sasakyan namin na tricycle. 

May time before na nagdadala kami ng mga crayons at notebook para sa aming Sunday school. Anjan din ang pagsasaulo ng mga verses. Ready dapat para kapag pinagrecite ay handa din.

Tanda ko dati noong malaki-laki na ako, siguro graders pa din naman, nagtatambourine naman ako. From time to time, Nagspecial number kami ni Ken ng kanta. Glory to God. Noon hindi ko pa ito maintindihan. Nabili lagi ang inay ng mga casette tape noon sa PCBS tapos ipapasaulo sa amin ang kanta. 

Ang sarap balikan!

I thank God for my parents, kung hindi dahil sa kanila, yung love ko sa Lord hindi magiging ganoong katibay. Nagstart ito because naging lifestyle na din nga.  Hindi ko nga maisip na kung wala ako sa Lord, paano na nga kaya ako? Ay grabe!! Iba kapag nakilala mo na personally ang Lord bilang Savior mo at alam mo na andyan lang siya parati. 
​
Serving transformed me and even ngayon na mga magulang na kami. 

Anong sarap na pati asawa ko, kasama ko sa pagseserve sa Lord. And goes with our children. 

As a parent, one of the biggest things we can do to help our children connect with God and with our church is to model and encourage a lifestyle of serving. 

 Find serving opportunities as soon as possible
Ang sarap magtanim ng seed sa mga bata. My prayer is for them to have a servant’s attitude in their hearts.  Is good to encourage them to offer to help sa kahit sinong tao basta kaya nila.  Always look for ways they can begin serving others.

Invite your children to serve with you
Kapag may pagkakataon, encourage them to serve regularly. If may mga special number, go lang, isama sila :) And explain to them, the REASON why we are doing it. Mainly to glorify GOD and nothing else. 

I believe, with their young age, may inilalagay na ang Lord sa kanilang mga puso. That only GOD can transform their lives forever. 

I thank God for the churches na naging kasama namin mula noon hanggang ngayon. God knows our heart. The desire to serve and wala ng iba pa. 

Currently, we are now here in Vienna, and patuloy na nagpapagamit. From Jesus Our FIRM Foundation (Batangas), Every nation (Singapore), then ngayon, sa JIL Church Austria. 

Glory to God! Napakabuti ng Lord kasi never Niya kaming iniwan. Laging sinasamahan. Lord, thank You po sa mga taong ginagamit nyo para kami po ay pagpalain dito sa lugar na ito. Lord, ang prayer namin ay patuloy na magamit mo. Ang lahat ay para sa kapurihan ng iyong pangalan. Salamat Lord. 

Glory to God for this children. Glory to God for this little family.

Be Intentional

8/6/2022

0 Comments

 
 Activities That I Can Do With Yanah and Yuri

Nakita ko lang itong mga ito sa isang site, and it's good na magawa din nga :)

1. Ask them what their day was like. Be specific. Ask them what they are learning in math, rather than, “How is math going?”
2. Hold your child’s hands in your hands and pray with them.
3. Ask them if they are having any problems, (with teachers, classes, friends, etc.), or struggling with anything, without getting too personal.
4. Play a short game with them. (Could be something like “20 Questions”)
5. Challenge them to memorize a verse from the Bible, and let them choose a verse that you have to memorize, but it has to have the same number of words as the verse your child has to memorize!
6. Make dessert for each other! You make it for them, and they have to make something for you.
7. Work a puzzle together.
8. Bake something together. Cookies are always popular!
9. Learn something new together, such as some phrases from a different language.
10. Swap chores. You do something that is one of your child’s chores, and your child will do one of your chores.
11. Play a board game together.
12. Play an online game together.
13. Play pretend with your young child.
14. Play “Simon Says”, and take turns being Simon.
15. Read to your child, and/or have your child read to you.
16. Help them with homework.
17. Pray with them before bed.
18. Take them out for dinner, just the two of you.
19. Work together to create your own list of things you can do, when you spend time together!

Titus 2:7, Show yourself in all respects to be a model of good works, and in your teaching show integrity, dignity…

I Corinthians 11:1, Be imitators of me, as I am of Christ.

I Timothy 4:12, Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity.
